Lanterna

A friendly AI assistant that runs on free cloud models — or on your own local models if your PC is strong enough.

by mikybuilds · free and open source (MIT)

Download for Windows Install on Linux

What it does

  • Chats with free models from Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Cerebras, Mistral and GitHub Models, using your own free keys. When one model reaches its limit, the next one answers.
  • Runs models on your own graphics card with Ollama, if your computer can handle them.
  • Remembers what you ask it to, sets reminders, searches the web, reads files and photos, and checks the weather.
  • Speaks English, Ukrainian and Italian.

Your data

Chats, memory and reminders are stored on your computer. There are no ads, no tracking and no account with Lanterna. If you choose to sign in with Google, Lanterna keeps an encrypted copy of your chats in a hidden folder of your own Google Drive, so you can use them on your other devices. Read the privacy policy.
