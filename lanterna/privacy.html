Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Lanterna is a free, open-source desktop app (Windows and Linux) made by mikybuilds. It has no servers of its own: it runs on your computer, and its author does not receive your chats, your files or anything about you. The full source code is public, so you can check everything written here.

What stays on your computer

  • Your chats, the facts Lanterna remembers about you, your reminders and your settings are stored in a local database in your user folder.
  • Your API keys are stored in your system's secure vault (Windows Credential Manager, GNOME Keyring or KWallet), never in plain files or logs.

What is sent, and where

  • Your messages go only to the AI service you chose (for example Groq or Google Gemini), under that service's own terms, together with the facts you asked Lanterna to remember. With local models (Ollama) nothing leaves your computer.
  • Web search, web pages and weather: when you ask for them, Lanterna sends the search words or city to DuckDuckGo, the page's website or Open-Meteo.
  • Update check: when Lanterna starts, it asks GitHub which version is the latest. Nothing about you is sent. You can turn this off in Settings → Updates.
  • Working folder (optional): if you choose a folder in Settings (or for one chat, under the message box), the files the assistant opens there are sent to the AI service in use, like a message. Lanterna sees nothing outside that folder and changes files only after you press Confirm.
  • Limits: the numbers in Settings → Limits and in the bar under the message box are counted on your computer from the services' own answers. Nothing is sent for them.
  • Bug reports are never sent automatically. You see the whole text and send it yourself, by e-mail or as a GitHub issue.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

Both Google features are optional and separate: you can use one, both or none.

Chat sync

Signing in with Google lets you see the same chats on your other devices.

  • For this Lanterna asks for one permission only: drive.appdata — “See, create and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive”. This gives access to a hidden folder that belongs to Lanterna. Lanterna cannot see, open or change any other file in your Google Drive.
  • In that folder Lanterna keeps an encrypted copy of your chats, memory, reminders and settings. Your API keys are not copied there.
  • This data moves only between your devices and your own Google Drive. It is never sent to the author of Lanterna or to anyone else, never sold, and never used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • The sign-in token is stored in your system's secure vault on each device.

Google Drive connector

If you connect Google Drive in Settings → Connectors, you can ask the assistant to create folders and save files in your Drive.

  • For this Lanterna asks for one permission only: drive.file — “See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app”. Lanterna can see and change only the files and folders it created; it cannot see the rest of your Drive.
  • Every new folder or file is shown to you first and is created only after you press Confirm. Lanterna never deletes files in your Drive.
  • Files go directly from your computer to your own Google Drive. They are never sent to the author of Lanterna or to anyone else, never sold, and never used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Disconnect removes the permission and the token from your computer.

Lanterna's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

GitHub connector

Optional. If you connect GitHub in Settings → Connectors, you can ask the assistant to push files from the chat's folder to your repositories.

  • Lanterna uses the GitHub App Lanterna Assistant. You install it only on the repositories you choose, and it has one permission: Contents (read and write the files and commits of those repositories), plus the read-only metadata GitHub always adds. It cannot see other repositories, create or delete repositories, change settings or add people.
  • You sign in by typing a short code on github.com. No password is entered in Lanterna; the sign-in token is stored in your system's secure vault and is used only from your computer, directly with GitHub.
  • Every push is shown first (repository, branch, files, message) and happens only after you press Confirm. Files that look like passwords or keys are never pushed.
  • Commits are made in your name; Lanterna Assistant is always shown as a co-author (only a name on the commit, it gets no access). Your code and token are never sent to the author of Lanterna. GitHub shows the owner of a GitHub App which accounts installed it.
  • Disconnect removes the token from your computer. To remove the access on GitHub too: GitHub → Settings → Applications.

How to delete your data

  • On your computer: uninstall Lanterna and delete its folder (on Windows %LOCALAPPDATA%\Lanterna, on Linux ~/.local/share/Lanterna and ~/.config/Lanterna).
  • In Google Drive: in Lanterna choose Settings → Sign out and delete the copy, or open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Lanterna → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove Lanterna's access at any time: Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services → Lanterna → Delete all connections.

Children

Lanterna does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. The AI services you connect have their own age rules.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
